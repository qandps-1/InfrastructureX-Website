About Infrastructure X

Infrastructure X (Infra-X) was founded in 2025 in Phoenix, Arizona. Infra-X invests innovative infrastructure solutions through strategic operations, relationships and partnerships. Since its inception, the company has focused on developing sustainable development, leveraging cutting-edge technology to build resilient infrastructure for the future. With a team of industry experts, Infra-X is committed to driving economic growth and environmental stewardship through large-scale projects in energy, transportation, and urban development.

Our Mission

At Infra-X, our mission is to advance extreme value-driven infrastructure investments and solutions to support superior financial returns, local economic growth and community transformation. We are committed to pioneering development through cutting-edge strategic partnerships and responsible resource management. Our goal is to create lasting impact by integrating environmental stewardship with economic prosperity, ensuring that every project contributes to the long-term well being of the communities we serve while delivering exceptional value to our stakeholders.

Areas of Focus

At Infrastructure X, we specialize in strategic investments and projects across diverse infrastructure sectors.

Infra-X Crypto

Infra-X Crypto focuses on standard gas and cheap energy to power mining operations. This model partners with local governments to ensure productivity and sustainability.

Infra-X Minerals

Infra-X Minerals invests in international mines for copper, tin and cobalt. These resources support both mining and digital asset infrastructure.

Infra-X Power Generation

Infra-X Power Generation provides energy to underdeveloped regions. This supports community growth with reliable affordable energy.

Infra-X Data Centers

Infra-X Data Centers are placed near mineral mines for efficiency. They leverage geothermal power for secure digital storage operations.

Infra-X Compute

Infra-X is developing a next-generation computer chip. It will solve complex problems using advanced, innovative infrastructure.

Infra-X Digital Assets

Infra-X Digital Assets allocates capital to blockchain infrastructure. This includes funding decentralized platforms and custody solutions.
